# ECS v11 reference after fresh manual movement (2026-10-05)

The accepted fresh manual direction change altered real-match trajectories. The ECS v10 repository baseline therefore remained a historical reference, but its final state could no longer be compared directly with current gameplay. The performance gate correctly rejected that comparison at `final_hash`.

The v11 reference starts from commit `5f561b07d4948b9b0ba756566fa99cedc6700757` in an isolated worktree. Its recorded rollback changes only four ECS performance files: vector length, humanoid selection scratch, and humanoid-base selection scratch. Fresh-input gameplay remains identical in both binaries. The [v11 manifest](../optimization/baselines/ecs_v11.json), [rollback patch](../optimization/baselines/ecs_v11_perf_rollback.patch), and [generator](../optimization/diagnostics/ecs_reference_refresh_v11.py) authenticate source, compiler, binary and parent-v10 identities. The current verifier also authenticates the old v10 binaries before loading v11.

The generator independently reproduced the baseline and compared it to the optimized engine for seeds 42 and 43. Both seeds had identical input, warm, final, checkpoint and active-frame trajectories; independent instrumented profiles confirmed the allocation improvement. The baseline replay preflight then passed with 122 assertions and zero skips. Its compressed evidence is `../optimization/evidence/ecs_v11_preflight_20261005.json.gz`.

The formal interleaved cohort passed with 15 process pairs per seed, 1,590,027 assertions and zero skips. CPU geometric ratios (optimized/baseline) were 0.8376 for seed 42 and 0.8161 for seed 43; bootstrap 95% upper bounds were 0.8445 and 0.8241. Wall-time ratios were 0.8376 and 0.8161; median paired p99 ratios were 0.752 and 0.735. Full-cache allocations remained zero in both builds, and per-frame allocation count and bytes decreased. The complete compressed report is `../optimization/evidence/ecs_v11_formal_20261005.json.gz`.

The same gameplay change caused the tactical-state seed-42 full-physics route to exercise 792 non-goalkeeper actor observations and eight set-piece frames instead of 876 and four. Release and Sanitizer each reproduced the new 960-frame aggregate twice: 3,588 non-goalkeeper observations and 12 set-piece frames. The tactical gate now pins those exact values without lowering its minimum assertion count. Product rendering and release acceptance remain separate gates.
